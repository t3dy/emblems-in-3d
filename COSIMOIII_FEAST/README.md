# Cosimo III Feast Engine

An interactive court-banquet simulator inspired by Cosimo III de' Medici and late Medici food culture.

The project treats feasting as statecraft. The player tunes a ceremonial table by choosing a course, perfume, entertainment, etiquette regime, guest type, jasmine saturation, pharmacy secrecy, and splendor budget. The scores are intentionally political: prestige, debt, and secrecy.

Historical anchors used for the design:

- Cosimo III ruled Tuscany from 1670 to 1723.
- Francesco Redi and the grand ducal spezieria/pharmacy are tied to Cosimo's famous jasmine chocolate.
- The jasmine chocolate tradition was treated as a guarded elite recipe and a prestige object.
- Banquets, table manners, imported ingredients, glass, service, music, and spectacle functioned as rank-producing technologies in early modern courts.
