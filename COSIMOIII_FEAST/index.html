Design Brief

Feast As Statecraft

Build a ceremonial table for Cosimo III de' Medici. The goal is not abundance alone: the best banquet converts imported cacao, jasmine, pharmacy, etiquette, and music into political aura without letting expense or suspicion devour the court.

Jasmine Chocolate

A guarded court beverage becomes the playable center: cacao, fresh jasmine, pharmacy, and prestige are layered like a recipe and like a diplomatic code.

Banquet Politics

Every course is a signal. Imported ingredients increase aura, but the bill rises, and secrecy can turn refinement into paranoia.

Table Manners

Forks, glass, napkins, measured service, and staged music turn eating into choreography. The table is a machine for producing rank.

Historical anchors

Cosimo III ruled Tuscany from 1670 to 1723. The project draws especially on accounts of his courtly chocolate culture: Francesco Redi, the grand ducal pharmacy, jasmine infusion, secrecy, and the use of elite consumption as prestige-work for a small state.
